# Journal

Characters are drawn as one outline together with objects (an arm with the torso, a bartender with the counter). They are cut and bent with bones with a pinned seam — `common.md`, "Fused parts"; keep the character of motion the same across the whole batch. fr 30, loop 3–5 s.

## Lessons

- Don't make a reaction and an action small and short (swell below ~115 %, shift under ~15 px, rotation under ~5°, peak shorter than ~6 frames) → make them bigger and longer, so the peak is visible in at least 2 frames out of 16 (otherwise the action doesn't read). 2026-10-05, scene journal-man-studying-or-working-on-laptop, source: verify
- Don't build the story on the motion of a hand or fingers when the character is drawn as one contour → give the main event to separate contour symbols (logo, picture, heart), the hand only leads up to it (the bones of one contour bend little and twist). 2026-10-05, scene journal-man-studying-or-working-on-laptop, source: verify. Since 2026-10-06 the arm can be cut at the shoulder and bent up to 15–20° with a pinned seam; a symbol still stays the main event.
- Don't promise in the story what the rig won't show ("handed over the folder", "the palm sinks") → write the story for what really moves. 2026-10-05, scene journal-human-and-ai-robot-exchanging-information, source: verify
- Don't move a reaction symbol away from its place (the like flies off to the picture) → the reaction in place: hop 15–25 px and swell with a spring (otherwise it reads as a detached part). 2026-10-05, scene journal-woman-s-feed-and-the-social-media-algorithm, source: verify

## User bans

- Don't bend a whole character fused into one contour with bones without a cut (robotic movements, the contour breaks) → cut the fused parts (`cuts`) and bend the cut-off part with a pinned seam (`pin`). 2026-10-05, all Journal scenes; 2026-10-06 the ban "don't animate Journal" lifted by the owner's decision: cutting is built into the engine.

# Black Chalk

The style has no rules of its own yet: work by common.md, keep the character of motion the same across the whole batch. fr 30, loop 3–5 s.

## Lessons

- Don't make a reaction and an action small and short (swell below ~115 %, shift under ~15 px, rotation under ~5°, peak shorter than ~6 frames) → make them bigger and longer, so the peak is visible in at least 2 frames out of 16 (otherwise the action doesn't read). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars, source: verify
- Don't leave a full hold longer than ~15 frames → fill it with the next story beat or the life of the symbols (otherwise a dead pause). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-rocket-ship-launching-with-flame-trail-surrounded-by-sparkling-stars, source: verify
- Don't remove several symbols at once or at the loop seam → one by one with a step of 4–8 frames, the last one leaves before the seam, taking start into account (otherwise they go as a "bunch" and there is a jump at the seam). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-security-shield-protection-with-password-asterisks-overlay, source: verify
- Don't fade symbols out with opacity → collapse them by scale: swell to 118–124 %, then 0 over 4–6 frames (opacity is almost not used in our animations). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars, source: verify
- Don't throw a rocket/object across other parts → check that during the motion it doesn't run into neighboring symbols; the symbols give way (otherwise an overlap). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-rocket-ship-launching-with-flame-trail-surrounded-by-sparkling-stars, source: verify
- Don't build a "pose — hold — return" loop when the output loop starts from the middle of the pose (start) → a continuous wave without holds or start in the SVG pose (otherwise the return at the start of the loop reads as a sharp reset). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars, source: verify

## User bans

- Don't make sharp movements of large parts (shield, head) → smoothness like Plain: head turn 8–12 frames, deceleration longer than acceleration, spring 13–38 frames. 2026-10-05, scenes black-chalk-security-shield-protection-with-password-asterisks-overlay, black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars
- Don't let part lines intersect during motion (the password field runs onto the shield) → move so that the contours don't run onto each other. 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-security-shield-protection-with-password-asterisks-overlay
- Don't build the collaboration on jumps in turn → the dotted line runs continuously around the circle, the figures move in time with it. 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-team-collaboration-silhouettes-in-circle-indicating-group-discussion-process
- Don't give the rocket a jerk with a return → a continuous looped flight (that is a story too). 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-rocket-ship-launching-with-flame-trail-surrounded-by-sparkling-stars
- Don't drag out the tempo → shield faster, collaboration about twice as fast (shorter loop, shorter movements). 2026-10-05, scenes black-chalk-security-shield-protection-with-password-asterisks-overlay, black-chalk-team-collaboration-silhouettes-in-circle-indicating-group-discussion-process
- Don't make a sharp head movement of the astronaut in the middle of the loop → the head moves only smoothly, without quick extra turns and jumps between poses. 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars
- Don't have the astronaut's head look up, hold the pose and return (the return reads as a sharp reset, the loop is not felt) → the head sways continuously and slowly in a wave through the whole loop, without holds, the start and end of the loop in the same pose. 2026-10-05, scene black-chalk-space-explorer-outline-gazing-toward-ringed-planet-and-sparkling-stars

## Lessons

- Don't make a reaction and an action small and short (swell below ~115 %, shift under ~15 px, rotation under ~5°, peak shorter than ~6 frames) → make them bigger and longer, so the peak is visible in at least 2 frames out of 16 (otherwise the action doesn't read). 2026-10-05, scene main-seo-search-bar-with-paper-plane-and-marketing-icons, source: verify
- Don't leave a full hold longer than ~15 frames → fill it with the next story beat or the life of the symbols (otherwise a dead pause). 2026-10-05, scene main-car-with-location-pin-symbolizing-gps-navigation-and-route-tracking, source: verify
- Don't remove symbols at the loop seam → the last one leaves before the seam, taking start into account (otherwise a jump at the seam); scattered symbols leave one by one with a step of 4–8 frames, while the contents of one object (purchases in a basket) — all at once. 2026-10-05, scene main-coupon-voucher-with-percent-discount-sign-and-barcode, source: verify
- Don't spin wheels drawn as an oval in perspective → keep them still, show driving with bumps and body tilt (otherwise the wheels look broken). 2026-10-05, scene main-car-with-location-pin-symbolizing-gps-navigation-and-route-tracking, source: verify
- Don't drive a car sideways to its axis → move it along the line through the wheel centers (otherwise it looks dragged). 2026-10-05, scene main-car-with-location-pin-symbolizing-gps-navigation-and-route-tracking, source: verify

## User bans

- Don't start the coupon with the symbols already shown and don't drag out the tempo → the first frame is the coupon itself, the elements around appear by the middle, at the end they quickly disappear; everything a bit faster. 2026-10-05, scene main-coupon-voucher-with-percent-discount-sign-and-barcode
- Don't run falling purchases behind the rim and under the basket handle → the purchases appear right inside the basket in turn (pop up in place), the basket responds; a checkmark at the end; at the end of the loop the purchases all disappear at once, not one by one. 2026-10-05, scene main-shopping-basket-with-groceries-and-checkmark-confirming-successful-purchase

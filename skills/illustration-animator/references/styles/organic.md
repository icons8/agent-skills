# Organic

The style has no rules of its own yet: work by common.md, keep the character of motion the same across the whole batch. fr 30, loop 3–5 s.

## Lessons

- Don't make a reaction and an action small and short (swell below ~115 %, shift under ~15 px, rotation under ~5°, peak shorter than ~6 frames) → make them bigger and longer, so the peak is visible in at least 2 frames out of 16 (otherwise the action doesn't read). 2026-10-05, scene organic-truck-delivery-vehicle-with-green-cargo-container-and-two-wheels, source: verify
- Don't remove several symbols at once or at the loop seam → one by one with a step of 4–8 frames, the last one leaves before the seam, taking start into account (otherwise they go as a "bunch" and there is a jump at the seam). 2026-10-05, scene organic-premium-account-upgrade-arrow-with-crown-and-confirmation-tick, source: verify
- Don't fade symbols out with opacity → collapse them by scale: swell to 118–124 %, then 0 over 4–6 frames (opacity is almost not used in our animations). 2026-10-05, scene organic-premium-account-upgrade-arrow-with-crown-and-confirmation-tick, source: verify
- Don't let reactions pop up without a visible cause → the press or gesture first, large (arm rotation from the shoulder 10–20° with anticipation), the reactions after. 2026-10-05, scene organic-online-personality-engaging-audience-with-likes-and-follower-add-sign, source: verify
- Don't promise a change of brightness or color (the engine doesn't change them) → show the state by motion: drooped — squashed and tilted, lit up — hopped and swelled. 2026-10-05, scene organic-brainstorm-lightbulb-burst-surrounded-by-energetic-black-lines, source: verify
- Don't depict a step or a run of a figure whose legs are not separated from the body (one contour) → move it as a whole: poke out and hide, hop, tilt (otherwise "running on one leg"). 2026-10-05, scene organic-exit-door-symbol-with-green-figure-walking-outward-quickly, source: verify

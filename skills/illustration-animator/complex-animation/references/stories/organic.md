# Organic stories

## organic-conversation-bubbles-between-two-silhouettes-indicating-dialogue-and-communication
Objects: a green and an outlined silhouette, speech bubbles with dots.
Story: the two talk: bubbles pop up above them in turn, swap places and pop away; the silhouettes bob as they speak.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - two bubbles out at once.

## organic-customer-satisfaction-meter-arrow-pointing-toward-happy-smiley-face-rating
Objects: a semicircle meter with sad, neutral and happy faces, a needle.
Story: the needle swings between the faces; the face it points at reacts (the sad one frowns harder, the neutral one twists), and it settles on the happy face, which smiles wider.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - the needle on the happy face.

## organic-data-backup-cloud-icon-uploading-multimedia-files-and-digital-content
Objects: a cloud with an up arrow, file icons (photo, globe, note, mail), dots, speed lines.
Story: the files fly up into the cloud along speed lines, the cloud swells as it swallows them, new files pop in below and follow.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - the cloud swollen.

## organic-generative-ai-process-linking-image-and-coding-icons
Objects: a big sparkle star in a circle of arcs, an image, a note, a text cursor, a code tag.
Story: the sparkle pulses; the icons around it in turn wobble, squash and transform, as if the AI reworks them.
Shape: cycle · 3 s · peak - an icon mid-transformation.

## organic-hands-giving-high-five-gesture-expressing-celebration-and-mutual-support

## organic-kanban-board-columns-with-task-cards-and-checkmark

Shape: cycle · 6 s · peak - the card dropped and the checkmark out.

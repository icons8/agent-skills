# Marginalia stories

## marginalia-a-person-sitting-in-front-of-a-computer-screen
Objects: a person from behind on a yellow chair, a dark monitor with colourful code lines, code tags (`</>`, `{ }`, `if`, `else`, `var`).
Story: the person types; code tags pop out around the monitor one after another, drift a little and pop away while new ones appear.
Shape: cycle · 5 s · peak - several tags out at once in the middle.

## marginalia-celebration-background
Objects: confetti pieces, squiggles, streamers.
Story: a burst of confetti scatters out, pieces fall and spin, thin out and vanish; a short empty pause; a new burst starts.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · peak - the burst at the start.

## marginalia-colorful-falling-confetti-for-party
Objects: confetti pieces, squiggles, streamers.
Story: same as the celebration background: a burst, fall, thinning, pause, new burst from the side.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · peak - the burst.

## marginalia-couple-holding-map-pin-with-checkmark-location-access-approval-and-geolocation
Objects: a man and a woman, a big green outline map pin with a checkmark.
Story: the two lift the big pin between them; the checkmark collapses into a dot, the pin stretches wide and flips into a V as they pull it apart, then shrinks back and the checkmark draws again.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · peak - the pin stretched widest.

## marginalia-cybersecurity-access-control-with-user-unlocking-password-padlock
Objects: a person with a yellow key, a browser window, a padlock, password dots.
Story: the person walks up, inserts the key and turns it; password dots type in one by one; the shackle springs open.
Shape: vignette · 4 s · peak - the lock opens.

## marginalia-ecological-problems
Objects: an octopus trapped in a plastic bottle, fish, seaweed, bubbles.
Story: the octopus drifts and squirms inside the bottle, the bottle bobs and rotates in the water; fish look on, seaweed sways, bubbles rise.
Shape: cycle · 5 s · peak - the bottle tilted furthest.

## marginalia-man-and-woman-in-self-driving-car
Objects: a pink car, a woman, a man reading a blue book, roadside plants.
Story: the car bobs on the road; the man reads the book and lifts his head to look around, then goes back to reading; plants pass by.
Shape: cycle · 6 s · peak - the man looking around.

## marginalia-man-doing-lunges-with-a-barbell
Objects: a man with a barbell overhead, weight plates.
Story: from a lunge the man presses up, straightens with the barbell high, his face strains, then lowers back into the lunge.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - standing at full height.

## marginalia-office-worker-at-desk-with-piles-of-documents-workplace-efficiency-and-productivity
Objects: a woman at a laptop, stacks of documents, a waste basket.
Story: she types; sheets fly off the laptop one by one and drift down onto the piles.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - several sheets in the air.

## marginalia-office-worker-sitting-at-desk-covering-mouth-with-no-comments
Objects: a man at a desk with a laptop, a potted plant.
Story: the man sits with a hand over his mouth, looks around, then lifts both arms in a shrug-stretch and returns to covering his mouth.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - arms up.

## marginalia-person-with-big-key-entering-large-keyhole-safe-login-and-secure-access
Objects: a person carrying a huge yellow key, a big black keyhole.
Story: the person walks towards the keyhole carrying the key; a draw-on line swoops from the key to the hole.
Shape: cycle · 1.3 s · peak - the step with the key forward.

## marginalia-person-with-watering-can-watering-a-tree
Objects: a person with a watering can, a young round tree, wind turbines.
Story: the person tilts the can, water drops fall to the tree, the tree sways and swells a little; turbines spin behind.
Shape: cycle · 3 s · peak - the can tilted most.

## marginalia-sleeping-in-bed-with-dog-nearby-peaceful-rest-and-relaxation
Objects: a man sleeping in bed, a dog under the bed, an alarm clock, a bookshelf.
Story: the man breathes slowly, "z" letters draw and float up; the dog peeks out; the clock wobbles gently.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - the dog peeks out.

## marginalia-tranquil-meditation-outdoors-stress-relief-and-wellness-concept-come-back-later
Objects: a meditating man on a mat, clouds, hills, plants.
Story: the man raises his arms slowly to the sides and lowers them as he breathes; hills fade in and out, clouds drift.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · peak - arms spread.

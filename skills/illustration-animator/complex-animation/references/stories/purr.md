# Purr stories

## purr-arms-holding-up-a-cat
Objects: two hands holding a tabby cat under its arms, motion lines.
Story: the cat hangs, dangling; its legs and tail swing as the hands bob it slightly; motion lines flicker by the hands.
Shape: cycle · 3 s · peak - the tail swung furthest.

## purr-cat-cleaning-itself-next-to-sleeping-cat
Objects: a spotted cat washing, a tabby cat sleeping, "z" letters.
Story: the spotted cat licks its chest in short repeats, its head bobbing; the tabby breathes in sleep and "z" letters float up.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - the "z" letters out.

## purr-cat-cleaning-itself-with-tongue
Objects: a spotted cat with a raised hind leg.
Story: the cat leans in and licks its belly near the tail, the tongue flicks out in repeats, then it lifts its head with closed eyes and leans in again.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · peak - the tongue out.

## purr-order-completed-cat-in-a-cardboard-box
Objects: a cat in an orange cardboard box.
Story: the cat turns around inside the box, showing its back, and turns back to face us; the box wobbles with it.
Shape: cycle · 1.5 s · peak - the cat's back to us.

## purr-tabby-cat-is-sleeping-downtime-or-come-back-later-page
Objects: a curled-up tabby cat, "z" letters.
Story: the cat breathes slowly, its body swelling and settling; "z" letters appear one by one and float up.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - three "z" letters out.

## purr-voracious-cat-with-empty-pet-bowl
Objects: a tabby cat lying on its back, an empty bowl.
Story: the cat lies by the empty bowl, flicks its tail and paws, looks sideways at the bowl.
Shape: cycle · 5 s · peak - the tail flick.

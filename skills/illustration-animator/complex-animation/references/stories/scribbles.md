# Scribbles stories

## scribbles-abstract-ink-line-texture
Objects: a cluster of small ink dabs.
Story: the dabs boil - they tremble in place as if redrawn every few frames.
Shape: cycle · 0.3 s · peak - none (constant boil).

## scribbles-abstract-ink-marks
Objects: three curved brush strokes.
Story: the strokes paint on one after another along their length, hold, then wipe off from the start; the loop restarts from empty.
Shape: cycle · 1.3 s · peak - all three strokes full.

## scribbles-circular-line-pattern
Objects: a scatter of small dots and rings.
Story: the dots boil in place.
Shape: cycle · 0.3 s · peak - none (constant boil).

## scribbles-hand-painted-black-strokes
Objects: a zigzag brush stroke and a thin line.
Story: the strokes paint on, hold, then wipe off; a short empty moment, then they paint on again.
Shape: cycle · 1 s · peak - both strokes full.

## scribbles-irregular-dark-paint-stain
Objects: a black paint stain.
Story: the stain boils, its edges trembling.
Shape: cycle · 0.3 s · peak - none (constant boil).

## scribbles-loose-flowing-line-strokes
Objects: four short diagonal brush dabs.
Story: the dabs wipe off one by one, the frame stays empty for a moment, then they paint back on in turn.
Shape: cycle · 1 s · peak - all dabs full.

## scribbles-splashes-of-black-ink-in-shape-of-crown
Objects: a crown-shaped ink splash.
Story: the splash boils in place.
Shape: cycle · 0.3 s · peak - none (constant boil).

## scribbles-tangled-line-texture
Objects: a group of square ink dabs.
Story: the dabs boil in place.
Shape: cycle · 0.3 s · peak - none (constant boil).

## scribbles-thin-ink-line-accents
Objects: a single ink loop line.
Story: the line draws from top down into a loop, holds, then wipes off; empty, and it draws again.
Shape: cycle · 1 s · peak - the loop complete.

## scribbles-unstructured-linework
Objects: a thin hand-drawn closed blob outline.
Story: the outline draws itself around, holds, then erases from the start around the shape; empty, and again.
Shape: cycle · 1 s · peak - the outline closed.

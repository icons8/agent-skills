# Stories: Cubes (10)

Common: blocky flat characters in purple, lime, pink and beige; everyday actions with clear rhythm.

## cubes-man-doing-a-lot-of-things-at-the-same-time
Objects: man sitting cross-legged with many arms: phone, pen, laptop, cup, glasses.
Story: his arms juggle the tasks - a phone call, writing, typing; small icons pop around him.
Shape: cycle · 5 s.

Shape: cycle · 5 s.

# Stories: 8bit (12)

Common to all: a flat character in a big full-body action that never stops; pixel icons around rock and bob slowly out of phase. Turns of the body and head are done with replacement drawings.

## 8bit-lettering-thank-you-with-likes-and-sparkles-text
Objects: pixel "THANK YOU!" lettering, thumbs-up, pixel heart, sparkles.
Story: the letters bob, the thumb and the heart pulse, sparkles twinkle, thin swirl lines draw themselves around the text.
Shape: cycle · 2 s · no character.

## 8bit-space-exploration-mission-and-technology
Objects: astronaut, satellite, pixel stars, comet.
Story: the astronaut floats in zero gravity, tumbling slowly and reaching toward the satellite; the satellite turns, the stars twinkle, the comet drifts.
Shape: continuous cycle · 6 s.

## 8bit-woman-achieving-work-life-balance
Objects: woman in a lotus pose, shadow, icons: burger, calendar, clock, gamepad.
Story: she levitates in the lotus pose and turns around her vertical axis (front → profile → front, replacement drawings); the shadow shrinks and grows; the icons orbit-bob around her.
Shape: continuous cycle · 4 s.

## 8bit-woman-controls-a-smart-home-system
Objects: woman lounging on a cloud sofa with a phone; icons: home, lock, bulb, play/pause.
Story: she lies back, raises her arm with the phone and taps; the play/pause icon toggles; the smart-home icons bob.
Shape: vignette · 4 s · peak: the tap.

## 8bit-woman-ice-skating-with-a-gift
Objects: woman on skates holding a gift, small fir tree, ornaments.
Story: she skates in strides with leg kicks, going away into the depth (getting smaller) and coming back; the tree and the ornaments pass by.
Shape: continuous cycle · 2 s.

## 8bit-woman-putting-money-in-a-digital-wallet
Objects: woman with a big coin, stairs, pink digital wallet.
Story: she climbs the stairs carrying the coin, jumps at the top and puts the coin into the wallet; back to the start.
Shape: vignette · 4 s · peak: the coin in the wallet.

## 8bit-woman-shopping-and-paying-by-card
Objects: woman holding up a bank card, shopping bag, clothes, burger and drink icons.
Story: she jumps for joy with the card raised; the bag swings; the clothes and food icons pop around her.
Shape: quick jump cycle · 1.5 s.

## 8bit-woman-shopping-online-on-phone
Objects: woman with a phone, star, % sign, basket.
Story: she dances: throws her arms up, jumps, the print on her shirt changes (heart ↔ smiley); the star, % and basket bob.
Shape: continuous cycle · 6 s.

## 8bit-young-man-bringing-a-bottle-for-recycling
Objects: young man carrying a bottle, recycling bin, thumbs-up.
Story: he runs in place toward the bin with the bottle (a run cycle); the bin and the thumbs-up rock.
Shape: run cycle · 2 s.

## 8bit-young-woman-giving-a-presentation
Objects: woman at a podium with a microphone, screen with bars and a pie chart, counters "+10", "+20".
Story: she points at the screen and talks; the bars grow, the counters tick up, the pie turns; her head turns between the audience and the screen.
Shape: vignette · 6 s · peak: the tallest bar.

## 8bit-young-woman-opens-a-box-of-christmas-decorations
Objects: woman, big box with a winter print, fir tree, snowflakes, ornaments.
Story: she lifts the box lid, confetti and sparkles burst out, she peeks in, the lid closes; snowflakes drift.
Shape: vignette · 4 s · peak: the burst.

## 8bit-young-woman-protects-inbox-from-spam
Objects: woman, shield, flying spam envelopes, key-arrow, grass.
Story: spam envelopes fly at her, she raises a big shield and the envelopes bounce off; she lowers the shield.
Shape: vignette · 3.3 s (60 fps) · peak: the shield up.

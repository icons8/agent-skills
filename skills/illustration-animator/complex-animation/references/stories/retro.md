## retro-flip-phone-and-a-magic-ball
Objects: a flip phone, a magic 8-ball keychain, a lipstick, a smiley, a crown doodle, sparkles.
Story: the phone rocks and turns slowly, the 8-ball swings on its ring and its answer window changes; sparkles twinkle.
Shape: cycle · 6 s · peak - the phone turned furthest.

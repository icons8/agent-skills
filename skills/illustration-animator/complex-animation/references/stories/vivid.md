# Vivid stories

## vivid-business-woman-giving-a-presentation-in-an-office
Objects: a woman in an orange blazer, a flip chart with a bar chart, a window with a city.
Story: she gestures at the chart with one hand, turns her head to the audience, then points at the chart again.
Shape: cycle · 6 s · peak - pointing at the chart.

# Project stories

## project-ai-converting-spoken-language-into-written-text
Objects: a violet chip-brain, a speech bubble with a sound wave, a converter arrows tile, a text page.
Story: the sound wave bars pulse and run; the converter arrows flip; lines appear on the text page.
Shape: cycle · 4 s · peak - the wave fully lit.

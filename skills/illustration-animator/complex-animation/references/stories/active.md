# Stories: Active (14)

Common to all: bright outlined characters in everyday situations; the face acts (blinks, smiles, mouth) the whole time; the body moves softly; a symbol supports the beat.

## active-avatars-of-happy-friends
Objects: three round avatars: a woman making a heart with her hands, a man blowing a party horn, a man showing a V sign.
Story: each friend acts in their circle: the woman pulses the hand-heart and smiles, the horn unrolls and rolls back, the V-sign hand waves; heads bob.
Shape: cycle · 2.3 s.

## active-creating-a-website-with-a-bot-assistant
Objects: robot assistant head, browser window with a picture and a pen, code tags.
Story: the robot pops out from behind the window, looks around and blinks, ducks back; the pen draws in the picture.
Shape: vignette · 4 s.

## active-freelancer-sitting-with-laptop-and-pointing-at-idea-bulb-creativity-and-brainstorming

Shape: vignette · 4.8 s · peak: finger up with the bulb.

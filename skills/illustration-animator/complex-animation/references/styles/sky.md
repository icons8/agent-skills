# Sky

Preliminary: measured on 3 Icons8 Sky animations with their source SVGs (Lottie). Treat the numbers as a hint; refine when more files arrive.

Sky is monochrome blue: thin blue outlines with flat fills in two or three tones of light blue, white highlights, no other colours. Subjects: a man presenting a chart on a video call with a cat in his lap, a woman lounging under the sun with a cocktail, a worker at a laptop with task cards. 3 of 3 have characters.

## Format

- **30 fps**. Loops **long: 4.5–6 s**. Short holds (3–7 %).

## Motion

- Rotation swings about 30 frames at ~10° (5–21°); moves small (~9 px), slow; scale rare.
- Calm everyday actions: a hand gestures at a chart, the woman sips and stretches, the sun rotates slowly, a progress ring fills, cards slide.
- Replacement drawings in all 3 (few, up to 6); no fades or pops.
- Lines draw themselves in 2 (chart lines, a progress ring).

## Easing

- Easy Ease 42 %, linear 11 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Outlines are live strokes (3 of 3) - outline before export.
- Morph (3 of 3) - needs a pose; draw-on - replace with a mask wipe.

## Differences from common.md (measured in this style: a hint; the bans and lessons of common.md and of this style still win)

- Long, calm loops with small slow swings.

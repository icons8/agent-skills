# Handy

Measured on 7 Icons8 Handy animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Handy is **1930s rubber-hose cartoon**: objects come alive as mascots with pie-cut eyes, big grins, white-gloved noodle arms and legs - a printer, a shredder, a folder, a cloud, a drone box, a TV robot reading a book, a banknote juggling a coin. Muted retro palette with soft shading.

## Format

- 30 fps. **Short cycles of 1–3 s** (two are exactly 1 s). Never still.

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 14 (9–18) | 11° (7–20°) |
| Move swing | 15 (9–20) | 35 px (13–89) |
| Scale swing | 11 (10–17) | 20 % (7–54 %) |

- **Classic rubber-hose bounce**: the whole body bobs and squashes on the beat, arms swing like hoses, the face pulls expressions (morph in 7 of 7) - eyes roll, mouth opens and grins.
- Gags: the printer feeds and throws sheets with its arms, the shredder cranks and the duck drawing goes in, the folder walks and gets the idea bulb, the TV robot reads and drools, the banknote tosses a coin that makes faces.
- Lines and details draw on (6 of 7); replacement drawings in 5 of 7 (up to 38).

## Easing

- Linear 45 % (frame-by-frame poses), Easy Ease 22 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Rubber-hose arms map well to bones with several joints and lag; the squash-bounce to `bounce`/`spring` with scale.
- Facial expressions need poses. Strokes in all 7 - outline before export.

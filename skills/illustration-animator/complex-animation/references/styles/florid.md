# Florid

Measured on 30 Icons8 Florid animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Florid is glossy gradient illustration with fine dark outlines: aqua-to-lilac and pink-to-yellow gradients, people with soft rounded hands, and tech/finance objects (cloud services, AI brain, crypto wallets, dashboards, VR, medicines). Polished and smooth.

## Format

- 30 fps (2 at 60). Loops **4–6 s** (median 5 s; from 1.7 to 7 s). Almost never still (6 of 30 have tiny holds).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 24 (12–48) | 9° (5–18°) |
| Move swing | 35 (15–74) | 16 px (8–31) |
| Scale swing | 31 (8–78) | 20 % (6–50 %) |

- Smooth, gentle motion: people type, gesture, tie a bow, read; hands are big and expressive (game controller, envelope, drawing tablet).
- **Fades are the main way UI appears**: 417 fades of UI cards, checks, notifications and code lines; 30 instant swaps; pops from 0 in 7 scenes.
- Objects float and turn: cards slide out of a phone, coins fly, candles of a chart bounce, a cloud pulses with data.
- Lines draw themselves (15 of 30); replacement drawings in 22 of 30 (up to 47).

## Easing

- Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 40 %, linear 15 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (30 of 30) carried. Strokes in all 30 - outline before export.
- Smooth UI fades and floats fit the engine well.

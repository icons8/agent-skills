# Neo-Sakura

**Preliminary**: measured on 4 Icons8 Neo-Sakura animations with their SVGs.

Neo-Sakura is anime-flavoured flat illustration with fine outlines in navy blue, yellow, green and red, with textures: a builder, a girl with a huge dog at a bus stop in the rain, a man on coin stacks, a girl jumping into a sale.

## Format and motion

- **24–25 fps**. Short cycles: **1–2 s**, one 6 s scene.
- Rain and textures flicker as **frame-by-frame swaps**: 1708 instant opacity switches - rain streaks and hand-drawn details boil on every frame. 80 fades.
- Body motion is small and quick: rotations ~11 frames at ~5°; hair and clothes ripple.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Frame-by-frame rain and boiling textures are not possible in the engine (and `common.md` forbids line boil); use a few rain streaks sliding down on a cycle instead.
- Strokes in all 4 - outline before export.

# Grapy

Measured on 2 Icons8 Grapy animations with their SVGs.

Grapy is a **recolour of Boba**: the same two scenes (the skater, the traveller with a map) with the same animation and loop lengths (4 and 5.5 s), redrawn in purple, lilac and black with white fills. **Use `boba.md` for the motion rules.**

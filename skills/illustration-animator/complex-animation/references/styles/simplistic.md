# Simplistic

Measured on 22 Icons8 Simplistic animations with their source SVGs (Lottie).

**Simplistic is a recolour of Outline** (`outline.md`): the same 21 animations with the same timing (plus one extra scene: a monitor with charts and a magnifier), drawn with dark plum outlines and fills in indigo-violet, mustard yellow, coral and peach, with soft cream blobs behind the objects. The measurements match Outline: 30 fps, loops ~3 s (1–7 s), small rotations (~6°, 14 frames), long slow moves and scales of UI elements, lettering that writes itself (trim in 21 of 22), pops from 0 (105 in 9), fades 306.

Use `outline.md` for all rules; this file only overrides the palette and look.

## Differences from Outline

- Colour: violet, mustard, coral, peach instead of lilac and lime; cream background blobs (some scenes) that sit still.

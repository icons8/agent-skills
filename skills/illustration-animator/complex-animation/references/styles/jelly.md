# Jelly

Measured on 9 Icons8 Jelly animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Jelly is soft, blobby jelly-bean monsters in flat red, teal, blue, yellow and black with tiny legs and big expressive eyes: a monster dribbling a ball, a lovesick blob going on a date, a thumbs-up hand-creature, living "404" digits, a muscle-man who pumps up. Silly and squishy.

## Format

- **All 9 files are exported at 999 fps** (an export setting; the motion is normal speed) - convert to 30 fps. Loops **2–3 s** (1.3–4 s). Never still.

## Motion

| What | Frames (at 30 fps) | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 12 (10–65) | 36° (20–55°) |
| Move swing | 12 (10–48) | 81 px (24–167) |

- **Big, squishy, fast motion**: the whole body bounces, leans and swings 20–55° in 10–12 frames - the blobs wobble like jelly.
- Comic transformations by replacement drawings (7 of 9, up to 196): the muscle-man shrinks to a tiny blob and pumps back up, the loading-bar monster runs and says "hey!", the "404" digits come alive and look around.
- Instant swaps (58) and fades (48); no pops.

## Easing

- Linear 53 % (frame-by-frame), Easy Ease 18 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- The squash-and-wobble body maps to `bounce`/`spring` with big scale and rotation; transformations need extra drawings.
- Strokes in all 9 - outline before export.

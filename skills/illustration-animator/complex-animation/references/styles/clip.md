# Clip

Measured on 11 Icons8 Clip animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps (the files are 24 fps), ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Clip is clean, airy flat illustration in navy, sky blue and light grey with small red and yellow accents: slim people around big screens, boards and charts (education, UI design, finance, voting, teamwork). The animation is calm and slow, with clear holds.

## Format

- **24 fps**. Loops **4–7.5 s** (median 5.5 s) - long and unhurried.
- **Holds are part of the style**: 8 of 11 have still frames (up to 17 % of the loop).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 44 (19–80) | 8° (5–17°) |
| Move swing | 58 (30–100) | 10 px (6–33) |

- People move slowly and little: a nod, a gesture at the board, a lean. The screen content does the work: a progress bar fills, formulas and a graph appear on the blackboard, a chart's bars grow, a scoreboard changes.
- **Fades are the main way things appear** (138 fades, 2 instant); a few pops from 0 (28 in 2 scenes - the graduation cap and the rocket).
- No replacement drawings (1 of 11).

## Easing

- Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 21 %, linear 12 %, the rest custom soft curves.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (11 of 11) are carried. Strokes in 8 of 11 - outline before export.
- A good match for the engine: slow transforms, fades and holds.

## Differences from common.md (measured in this style: a hint; the bans and lessons of common.md and of this style still win)

- Slow tempo is right here: 1.5–3 s per swing, holds of up to ~1 s, small amplitudes.
- Opacity fades are the normal way to show and hide.

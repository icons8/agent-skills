# Amani

**Preliminary**: measured on 1 Icons8 Amani animation with its SVG.

Amani is an elegant silhouette illustration: a slender black figure with vivid gradient plant leaves (orange, pink, teal). The motion is slow and graceful.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, loop 6 s. Never still, but very calm.
- Slow swings: ~49 frames (44–77), only ~4° (3–7°); the figure barely moves, ~8 px over 3+ s.
- The leaves sway and change shape gently (path morph on 28 of 30 layers).

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients - carried. Leaf shape changes - need a pose; without one, rock the leaves around their stems (±3–5°, 45–75 frames) in different phases.

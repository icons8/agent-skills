# Dazzle Line

Measured on 25 Icons8 Dazzle Line animations with their SVGs.

Dazzle Line is the **line version of Dazzle**: the same scenes and the same animation, redrawn in black outline with white fills and a single hot-pink accent. The loop lengths match Dazzle one to one, and the motion numbers are the same within noise (rotation swings 23 frames at 11°, moves 26 frames at 16 px, 85 pops from 0, Easy Ease 53 %).

**Use `dazzle.md` for all motion rules.** What differs is only the drawing: thin black lines everywhere (strokes in 25 of 25 - outline before export), one accent colour.

# Dazzle

Measured on 27 Icons8 Dazzle animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Dazzle is bold outlined flat illustration in electric purple, hot pink, lime and peach, scattered with confetti dashes and starbursts. Chunky characters with big hands and feet (relaxing at the office, skateboarding, high-fiving, meditating on coins) and busy object compositions (calendars, parcels, banks, maps, laptops). Energetic and celebratory.

## Format

- 30 fps. Loops **4–6 s** (from 1.7 to 6 s). Never still (one has holds of ~24 % - a coder pausing).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 23 (11–40) | 11° (5–19°) |
| Move swing | 29 (16–55) | 16 px (10–33) |
| Scale swing | 29 (8–59) | 20 % (8–60 %) |

- **Pops everywhere**: 95 pops from 0 in 15 of 27 - starbursts, envelopes, checks, gifts, coins pop in and bounce.
- **Confetti dashes and stars** twinkle and rotate around every scene.
- Characters do big comic actions: feet up on the desk and spin in the chair, kick-flip on a skateboard, a two-person high-five jump, a shout into a megaphone on a fitness ball.
- Lines draw themselves (14 of 27); replacement drawings in 21 of 27 (up to 63).

## Easing

- **Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 52 %**, linear 13 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Pops and decorative twinkles fit the engine's `pop` and `spin`; starbursts can rotate and scale.
- Strokes in all 27 - outline before export. Big comic poses use replacement drawings.

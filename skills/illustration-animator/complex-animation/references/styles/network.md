# Network

Measured on 21 Icons8 Network animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Network is clean tech illustration with blue and green gradients on soft blue blob backgrounds, black outlines on devices, small floating cubes: phones, laptops, browser windows, a drone, a robot, a factory arm, security and finance UI. No people (a robot at most).

## Format

- 30 fps. **Loop 4 s in 20 of 21** (one 5 s). Never still.

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Move swing | 30 (15–48) | 23 px (10–42) |
| Rotation swing | 15 (5–46) | 8° (4–15°) |
| Background breathing scale | 119 (a full 4 s cycle) | 30 % |

- **UI micro-stories inside a 4 s loop**: a face-scan mesh draws and a check appears, a letter slides out of an envelope, a magnifier rates a candidate with stars, a key turns in a folder lock, a card slides out of a phone after a fingerprint, a gauge needle swings, a sync icon spins.
- **Fades are the main tool** (221 fades, 17 instant): UI elements fade in and out; lines draw on in 9 of 21; small cubes float around.
- Few replacement drawings (9 of 21, up to 12).

## Easing

- **Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 73 %** (the highest of all styles), linear 5 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (21 of 21) carried. Strokes in 21 of 21 - outline before export.
- A good fit for the engine: short transforms with Easy Ease, fades and floats.

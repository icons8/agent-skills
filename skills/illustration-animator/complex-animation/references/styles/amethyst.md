# Amethyst

**Preliminary**: measured on 2 Icons8 Amethyst animations with their SVGs.

Amethyst is purple line art: thin black and violet strokes, light lilac fills, sketchy decorative details (dots, sparkles, constellation lines).

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, loops 4 s. Never still.
- Swings ~30 frames (15–30), rotations ~8° (6–14°), moves ~46 px (26–68 px); short pops of 6–15 frames at 25–57 %.
- **Line drawing is central**: hundreds of strokes (68 and 311) and many draw-on animations (trim, 42 and 106): constellation lines, sparkles and details draw themselves on and off.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes and draw-on lines - the engine needs outlined strokes and can't draw a line on. Without that, Amethyst loses most of its motion: use pops and quick fades of the details instead.

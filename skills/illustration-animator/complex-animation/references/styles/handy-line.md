# Handy Line

Measured on 7 Icons8 Handy Line animations with their SVGs.

Handy Line is the **line version of Handy**: the same seven mascot scenes with the same animation (same loop lengths, rotation swings 15 frames at 11°, moves 15 frames at ~34 px), redrawn in outline. **Use `handy.md` for the motion rules.** One file is exported at 200 fps; convert to 30 fps.

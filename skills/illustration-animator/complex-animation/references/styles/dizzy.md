# Dizzy

Measured on 6 Icons8 Dizzy animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Dizzy is soft flat illustration with fine outlines in warm cream, coral, sky blue and lilac: people working on laptops and tablets, students, a designer, data monitors. Calm and friendly.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Loops **4–5 s** (3–6 s). Never still.
- Rotations ~15 frames (10–34), ~11° (6–21°); moves ~37 frames, ~18 px.
- Screen content changes do much of the work: shapes on a design tablet change colour, code lines scroll, a chart grows, coins pop (24 pops in 3 scenes).
- Replacement drawings in all 6 (up to 154) - hands and heads switch poses.

## Easing

- Easy Ease 38 %, linear 29 % of authored keys.

- Strokes in all 6 - outline before export.

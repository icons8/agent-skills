# Lounge

Measured on 7 Icons8 Lounge animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Lounge is bold two-tone flat illustration: deep navy plus one strong accent (royal blue or coral pink), silhouette-like people (often seen from behind) at screens, a megaphone, a social post.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Loops **4–6 s**. Mostly continuous.
- **Slow and calm**: rotations ~44 frames (31–53) at ~8°; moves ~44 frames at ~11 px.
- A gesture or two per loop: a hand raised to the screen, the megaphone shouts, hearts pop on a post, password dots fill; very few pops (4) and no replacement drawings.

## Easing

- **Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 53 %**, linear only 3 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- A very good fit for the engine: slow transforms with Easy Ease, no swaps. Strokes in 5 of 7 - outline.

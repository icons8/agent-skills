# Bubble gum

Measured on 23 Icons8 Bubble gum animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Bubble gum is soft pastel outlined illustration (pink, lilac, yellow, peach) with round, rubbery characters: a ginger guy, a girl with a bun, plus objects (phones, monitors, shopping bags, coins, a megaphone, test tubes). The motion is springy and cartoony.

## Format

- 30 fps. Loops **3–4.5 s**; run and jump cycles of 1–1.7 s. Never still (2 of 23 have tiny holds).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 13 (8–23) | 11° (6–24°) |
| Move swing | 18 (8–32) | 28 px (10–56) |
| Scale swing | 12 (6–32) | 12 % (9–44 %) |

- **Full-body cartoon cycles**: running with a big envelope, flying superhero-style, crossing a finish line, carrying money - long arms and legs swing wide.
- **Objects act too**: the megaphone zooms toward the viewer and back, a coin flips on a growth graph, the hourglass flips, the email in a phone slides out and back.
- Pops from 0 (37 in 8) and fades (37); replacement drawings in 20 of 23 (up to ~380 in a run cycle).

## Easing

- Linear 44 % of authored keys (frame-by-frame poses), Easy Ease 28 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Big replacement-drawing cycles (runs, flights) - out of reach without extra drawings; build scenes from bends of cut limbs and a bouncing body.
- Strokes in all 23 - outline before export.

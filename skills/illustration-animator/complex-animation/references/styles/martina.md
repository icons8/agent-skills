# Martina

Measured on 7 Icons8 Martina animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Martina is friendly flat illustration with fine dark outlines in lilac, periwinkle blue and coral: young people working, thinking, chatting, playing guitar, relaxing with popcorn, holding a cat.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Loops **1.3–4.4 s** (median 2.7 s). Never still.
- Gentle acting: rotations ~19 frames (15–37) at ~8° (4–15°); the body barely moves (~10 px). Heads tilt, hands gesture, a guitar is strummed, a cat is stroked; faces change (morph in 7 of 7).
- Bubbles and icons scale in and out (chat bubbles with "?" and a thumbs-up, likes on a post); replacement drawings in 6 of 7.

## Easing

- **Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 55 %**, linear 5 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Small, soft motions fit cut limbs with bones; faces need poses. Strokes in all 7 - outline before export.

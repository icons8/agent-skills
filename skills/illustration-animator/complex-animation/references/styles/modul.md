# Modul

Measured on 9 Icons8 Modul animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Modul is soft isometric illustration in pale lilac and grey with violet accents: extruded 3D lettering on browser panels ("AI", "SEO", "SMM", "UX UI", "ORDER ONLINE", "CODE", "TAX"), floating windows, phones, coins. No people.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Loops **6 s** (4–6 s). Never still.
- Panels float and slide: moves ~44 frames (20–45) at ~35 px; few rotations (~20°) on cursors and coins.
- A cursor travels to the lettering and clicks; letters swap or flip in place (replacement drawings in 6 of 9, instant swaps 40); coins flip edge-on; gears turn.

## Easing

- Linear 52 %, Easy Ease 33 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Isometric letter flips and coin turns need poses (morph) or extra drawings; panel floats and cursor moves fit the engine.
- Strokes in all 9 - outline before export.

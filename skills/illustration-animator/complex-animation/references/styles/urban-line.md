# Urban Line

Measured on 10 Icons8 Urban Line animations with their source SVGs (Lottie). Sizes are on the 1200×1200 canvas, durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges are p25–p75.

**Urban Line is the line version of Urban** (`urban.md`): the same small slim people in wide scenes, drawn in dark navy only - solid navy clothes and hair, thin navy outlines, white everything else, the background a pale grey sketch. Half the scenes repeat Urban (the office meeting, the bitcoin investor, the designer in the space dome, yoga with a smartwatch, the courier), half are new (a drone delivering food, a parachutist falling towards crocodiles, a businessman at a closed 404 door, trying on clothes from a projection).

Use `urban.md` for the rules; the numbers match: 30 fps, loops 3–6.5 s, small slow gestures (rotation 7°, swing 25 frames), Easy Ease 58 %, holds in some scenes (up to 21 %).

## Differences from Urban

- **Light and colour change by fades** (1341 fades, mostly in one scene): day turns to night by darkening layers, a projected outfit flickers on and off; replacement drawings in 6 of 10 (up to 487 - flicker frames).
- Thin outlines are live strokes everywhere (10 of 10) - outline before export.

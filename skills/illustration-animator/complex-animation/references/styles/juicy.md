# Juicy

Measured on 26 Icons8 Juicy animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Juicy is bright, juicy flat illustration with thin outlines in yellow, hot pink, periwinkle and lime: stylish young people working, studying, shopping, celebrating, plus floating UI and objects. Lively everyday acting with a lot of detail.

## Format

- 30 fps. Loops **3–6 s** (median 4 s; 1.9–7 s). Mostly continuous; short holds in 8 of 26 (up to 16 %).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 27 (15–45) | 9° (5–15°) |
| Move swing | 33 (16–57) | 30 px (13–72) |
| Scale swing | 85 (43–141) | 30 % (17–30 %) - slow breathing scales |

- People act naturally: a creative person raises a lit bulb, a meditating worker throws her arms up as documents fly around her, a courier kicks on a scooter, paper planes fly out of a girl's phone, a student daydreams and leans back.
- **Lines draw themselves in almost every scene** (trim in 25 of 26): routes of paper planes, UI lines, scribbles, plans.
- Pops from 0 in 6 scenes (34); replacement drawings in 11 of 26 (one scene with ~1200 - a stream of cash).

## Easing

- Easy Ease 29 %, linear 22 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes in all 26 - outline before export; draw-on lines - not possible, use pops or fades.

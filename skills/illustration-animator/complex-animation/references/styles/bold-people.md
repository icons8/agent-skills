# Bold People

The style has no rules of its own yet: work by common.md, keep the character of motion the same across the whole batch. fr 30, loop length per common.md "Smoothness" (a simple scene - 2 s).

## Measured (1 animation, a sketch)

The hands type, reach for the mouse and lift the cup; hand poses are replacement drawings (194 short layers in the loop). Rotations ~39 frames (20–49) at ~33°, moves ~38 frames at ~24 px. A single file - treat as a sketch.

## Lessons

- Don't move characters as one rigid silhouette (tilt, squat, dance, run, jump) → bend arms, legs and torso by bones, otherwise it looks robotic (judge: 5 of 6 scenes). The figure is one contour (a stroke of ~10 thousand points and one fill), so first cut the moving limbs off with a pinned seam (`common.md`, "Fused parts": never bend a whole fused figure without a cut), then bend.

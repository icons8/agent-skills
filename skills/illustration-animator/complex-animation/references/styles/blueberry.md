# Blueberry

**Preliminary**: measured on 2 Icons8 Blueberry animations with their SVGs.

Blueberry is line art in deep blue-violet and black with light lilac accents: people at work, video calls, abstract data shapes, decorative orbit lines.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, loops 5.5–5.7 s. Never still.
- Calm, smooth swings: rotations ~31 frames (18–47), ~8° (4–13°); moves ~41 frames (29–60), ~22 px (13–30).
- People gesture and shift their weight; a figure balances on a data column, swaying with arms out; orbit lines draw (trim in both).
- Some replacement drawings (31 in the video-call scene).

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes (both) - outline before export; draw-on lines - not possible.

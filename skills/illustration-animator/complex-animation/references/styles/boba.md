# Boba

**Preliminary**: measured on 2 Icons8 Boba animations with their SVGs.

Boba is bold outlined characters in bright flat colours (sky blue, yellow, red, teal): teenagers and travellers in active poses.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, loops 4–5.5 s. Never still.
- Rhythmic swings: rotations ~30 frames (21–30), ~18° (12–27°); moves ~30 frames, ~22 px. Even half-second beats (15/30/45 frames).
- Whole-body action with no replacement drawings: the skater pushes and rides, the tourist flips the map and looks around.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes (both) - outline before export. Face and shape changes (morph in both) need poses.

# Kingdom

Measured on 10 Icons8 Kingdom animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps (the files are 24 fps), ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Kingdom is a quirky folk-tale style in coral red, cobalt blue and orange with spiky "lace" cuffs: royal hands with rings, a king, a queen under an umbrella, a mouse reading, a parachuting package. Playful and a bit absurd.

## Format

- **24 fps**. Loops **2–4.5 s** (up to 6). Mostly continuous; one has a 14 % hold (the padlock after it opens).

## Motion

| What | Frames (at 30 fps) | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 8 (8–15) | 8° (8–13°) |
| Move swing | 28 (9–71) | 21 px (7–87) |

- **Stepped, on-twos feel**: short swings of the same size (8 frames, 8°) repeat - a choppy, hand-made rhythm; linear 54 % of authored keys.
- Gags: the parachute folds and the package falls, the hair-dryer hood lifts and coins pour out, the king dances on coin stacks, the queen's umbrella blocks flying spam and then flips inside out, two hands swap a crown and a sceptre.
- Mostly fades (145) and instant swaps (28); replacement drawings in 8 of 10 (up to 64).

## Easing

- Linear 54 %, Easy Ease only 12 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- The choppy rhythm can be reproduced with `hold` easing between keys every 2–3 frames.
- Strokes in 9 of 10 - outline before export.

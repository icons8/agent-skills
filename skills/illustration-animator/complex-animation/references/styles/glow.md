# Glow

**Preliminary**: measured on 4 Icons8 Glow animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Glow is fine line art in periwinkle blue and navy with neon-lime highlights: tall, stylish people with laptops, robotic hands, virtual data panels, circuit lines.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Long loops: **6–8.3 s**. Mostly continuous (one with short holds).
- The body is calm: rotations ~12 frames (8–23) at ~7°; moves ~52 frames at ~10 px.
- **Screens and data flicker a lot**: 697 instant opacity switches and 112 fades - UI states, highlights and code blink on and off; circuit lines and charts draw themselves (trim in 4 of 4).
- Replacement drawings in all 4 (up to 111).

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes in all 4 - outline before export. Instant UI swaps are possible as hold keys on opacity of separate parts.

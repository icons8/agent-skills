# Neon

Measured on 4 Icons8 Neon animations with their SVGs.

Neon is a **recolour of Glow**: the same four scenes (the AR analyst, the colleagues on bar stools, the woman thinking with question marks, the robot hands at a laptop) with the same animation and loop lengths (6–8.3 s), drawn in neon cyan, hot pink and purple with gradients. **Use `glow.md` for the motion rules.**

# Jaconda

**Preliminary**: measured on 6 Icons8 Jaconda animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75.

Jaconda is gradient flat illustration with slender, elongated people (blue tops, brown trousers) on light grey platforms, with bold props: a giant "404" on a cart, a popcorn bucket, a big clock, a column chart, a giant monitor.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps. Loops **1.5–5 s**. Mostly continuous; one scene holds 13 %.
- Rotations ~16 frames (13–30) at ~10°; moves ~29 frames at ~18 px; big scale changes (~65 %) for props that pop or swell.
- Physical gags: a woman pushes the huge "404" on a cart, columns collapse in a financial decline while she floats away on a balloon, popcorn bursts out of a bucket, the clock hands spin; pops from 0 (24 in 3 scenes), instant swaps (16).

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (6 of 6) carried; strokes in 5 of 6 - outline before export.

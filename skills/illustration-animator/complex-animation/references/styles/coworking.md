# Coworking

Measured on 13 Icons8 Coworking animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Coworking is soft gradient illustration with grain in muted sage, lilac, pink and peach: people working from home and in studios (podcast, coding, shopping, support), finance and crypto objects, and chunky 3D-like lettering ("SALE", "BOOM!", "THANK YOU!").

## Format

- 30 fps (one at 60). Loops **4 s** (7 of 13), from 3 to 6 s. Short holds in 4 of 13 (up to 15 %).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 22 (12–54) | 11° (6–18°) |
| Move swing | 19 (9–45) | 16 px (7–25) |
| Scale swing | 29 (10–60) | 15 % (10–33 %) |

- Lettering scenes: the letters bob and rock one after another in a wave, sparkles twinkle.
- People: calm working motion - typing, talking into a mic, a head turn, a hand gesture; the face changes (morph in 13 of 13).
- Objects: a coin bounces on a crypto pedestal, cards slide in a wallet app, a taxi moves on a map.
- Mostly fades (97) and instant swaps (26); pops from 0 are rare (10 in 3).

## Easing

- Easy Ease 35 %, linear 30 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (13 of 13) carried. Strokes in 10 of 13 - outline. Lettering waves work well with the engine (per-letter rock and bob with offsets).

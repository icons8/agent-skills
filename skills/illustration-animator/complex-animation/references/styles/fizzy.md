# Fizzy

**Preliminary**: measured on 1 Icons8 Fizzy animation with its SVG.

Fizzy is soft gradient flat illustration with grain: a man in a purple hoodie recording a podcast at a laptop.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, loop 6 s. Calm: rotations ~29 frames at ~13°, moves ~7 px over 3 s. He talks and gestures with one hand, nods; a few fades (8).

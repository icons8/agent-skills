# Kit

Measured on 11 Icons8 Kit animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Kit is glossy pseudo-3D object compositions with gradients and soft shadows: devices (monitors, phones, POS terminal, tablets), cards, coins, robots, a face-ID head, a snow globe. No people (one robot). The motion is a slow, smooth product-style showcase.

## Format

- 30 fps. Loops **6 s** (7 of 11), 4–5 s for the rest. Never still.

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 30 (30–44) | 4° (2–12°) |
| Move swing | 40 (24–60) | 24 px (12–42) |
| Scale swing | 15 (8–67) | 15 % (9–64 %) |

- Objects float slowly with tiny rotations (half-second swings of 2–4°), like the Techny cycle but slower.
- On top: screen UI changes (charts, dashboards, payment screens), coins and particles pop (93 pops in 4 scenes), cards slide, the face-ID mesh scans the head, a receipt rolls out of the terminal.
- Replacement drawings in 10 of 11 (up to 261 - particle streams).

## Easing

- **Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 57 %**, linear 21 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (10 of 11) carried. Strokes in 11 of 11 - outline before export. The slow floats and screen fades fit the engine.

# Colors

Measured on 8 Icons8 Colors animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Colors is vivid gradient illustration with grainy texture and bold colour (emerald, coral, cobalt, yellow, lilac): dynamic people with big gestures - flying with a magnifier, surfing a credit card, balancing on charts - and tech objects (a rocket in a phone, a crypto counter).

## Format

- 30 fps (one at 90). Loops **5–6 s**, short ones 2 s. Never still.

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 24 (14–42) | 11° (5–18°) |
| Move swing | 19 (7–39) | 22 px (11–44) |
| Scale swing | 25 (8–59) | 32 % (10–81 %) |

- Energetic full-body action: the shopper rides a card like a skateboard and spins, the analyst sways on a bar chart with her arms out, the hiring manager flies over a folder with a giant magnifier.
- Objects: the phone with the rocket turns in the hand and the rocket blasts off, the crypto counter rolls through numbers, a cloud pulses with data.
- Replacement drawings in 6 of 8 (up to 169); opacity for instant swaps (40), no pops from 0.

## Easing

- Linear 34 %, Easy Ease 25 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Gradients (8 of 8) carried; grain textures are part of the drawing. Strokes in 8 of 8 - outline.
- Big full-body poses rely on replacement drawings - without them keep to bends, sways and object actions.

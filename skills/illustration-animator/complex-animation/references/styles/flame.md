# Flame

Measured on 12 Icons8 Flame animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps (the files are 24 fps), ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Flame is bold graphic illustration in deep navy, orange, yellow and sky blue, often on a navy blob: space (astronauts, planets, telescopes), brains with arms, cartoon heads, clouds and devices. Expressive and full of character, with a hand-printed feel.

## Format

- **24 fps**. Loops **2–4 s** for punchy scenes, 6–7 s for calm ones. Mostly continuous; a reveal can hold (one scene holds 38 % - the head after it closes).

## Motion

| What | Frames (at 30 fps) | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 22 (8–55) | 15° (9–28°) |
| Move swing | 60 (24–200) | 60 px (17–201) |
| Scale swing | 50 (10–96) | 20 % (10–49 %) |

- **Big transformations**: a brain jumps up in triumph with a burst of lightning, a head's top opens to show the tangled neurons and closes, a cloud opens its mouth and swallows a box of files, a unicorn dream pops out of a sleeper's head.
- Lots of particles and pops (150 pops from 0 in 4 scenes): sparks, lightning, stars, dots.
- Fades (217) and instant switches (51); replacement drawings in all 12 (up to 142).

## Easing

- Linear 26 %, Easy Ease 26 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Transformations (a head opening, a cloud's mouth) need poses or extra drawings.
- Strokes in 9 of 12 - outline before export.

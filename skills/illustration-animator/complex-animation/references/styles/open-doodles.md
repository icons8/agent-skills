# Open Doodles

**Preliminary**: measured on 2 Icons8 Open Doodles animations with their SVGs.

Open Doodles is loose hand-drawn ink doodles with navy fills and dotted patterns: people reading in relaxed poses.

## Format and motion

- 30 fps, **2 s** loops. Tiny, steady idle motion: limbs and head rock ~20° in 10-frame swings (the reading hand turns pages, the foot taps), leaves sway. Easy Ease 78 %.

# Cubes

Measured on 10 Icons8 Cubes animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Cubes is flat characters with simple blocky shapes in electric purple, lime green, pink and beige: people working, meeting, shopping on a giant phone, doing yoga, cradling a baby, buying a house. The animation is everyday acting with clear, rhythmic body motion.

## Format

- 30 fps. Loops **4–6 s** (median 5 s). Mostly continuous; short holds in 5 of 10 (up to 12 %).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 12 (7–27) | 10° (5–19°) |
| Move swing | 30 (15–69) | 14 px (8–26) |
| Scale swing | 8 (5–104) | 7 % (5–15 %) |

- Clear body actions: a yoga pose changes into a triangle and back, a man multitasks with many arms, a woman rocks a baby in a rocking chair, a shopper swipes clothes on a giant phone and kicks her leg.
- Lines draw themselves in every file (trim in 10 of 10): routes, cycle arrows, decorations.
- Replacement drawings in 9 of 10 (up to 30). Pops and fades rare.

## Easing

- Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 47 %, linear - 10 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes (10 of 10) - outline before export; draw-on lines - not possible.
- Body poses with moderate rotations (10°) fit cut limbs with bones; big pose changes (yoga) need extra drawings.

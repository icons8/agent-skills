# Bloom

Measured on 12 Icons8 Bloom animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Bloom is warm outlined characters in pastel and bright colours (orange, pink, lilac, blue), mostly young women and couples online: shopping, blogging, video calls, webinars, social media, meditation. The animation is lively social-media life: the character gestures and reacts while icons, hearts, bubbles and emoji pop around.

## Format

- 30 fps (one at 200 fps). Loops **5–6 s** (4–6 s). Mostly never still (2 of 12 have short holds).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 25 (12–47) | 7° (4–14°) |
| Move swing | 43 (25–117) | 24 px (8–46) |
| Scale swing | 36 (5–66) | 22 % (8–45 %) |

- The character acts: talks, waves, scrolls the phone, types, holds a selfie stick; the face changes (morph in 12 of 12).
- **Lots of pops**: hearts, likes, stars, bubbles with dots, social icons pop in and out around the character (54 pops from 0 in 4 scenes) and fade (72 fades).
- Replacement drawings in 8 of 12 (up to 34).

## Easing

- Easy Ease `[0.33, 0, 0.67, 1]` - 50 %, linear - 13 %.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Facial acting and hand poses need poses or extra drawings; the symbol pops and the body sway work in the engine as is.
- Strokes in all 12 - outline before export.

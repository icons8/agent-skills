# Droll

Measured on 10 Icons8 Droll animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Droll is a grotesque, comic-book cartoon: odd characters with huge noses, googly eyes and noodle limbs, thick black outlines and loud flat colours (yellow, blue, green, pink, orange) - a businessman in a wheelbarrow, a courier on a skateboard, a guy with a house for a head. The animation is fast, snappy slapstick.

## Format

- Short loops: **0.8–1 s** cycles for walks, rides and skating; up to 2.5–5 s for scenes with a gag.
- **7 of 10 files are exported at 900 fps** (an export setting; the motion itself is normal speed). Convert to 30 fps when reading their numbers.

## Motion

| What | Frames (at 30 fps) | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 6 (4–10) | 20° (6–39°) |
| Move swing | 6 (5–16) | 24 px (12–53) |

- **Very fast and snappy**: swings of 4–10 frames with big angles - the fastest of all styles so far.
- Walk and ride cycles with flailing limbs; faces pull exaggerated expressions (eyes pop, mouths gape - morph in 10 of 10); the house-head tilts and blinks.
- Replacement drawings in 8 of 10 (up to 49); a gag can pause (one scene holds for 15 %).

## Easing

- Linear 33 %, Easy Ease 28 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Strokes in all 10 - outline before export. Facial gags need poses. Fast snappy swings are fine in the engine (use short `turn`/`move` with `soft`, avoid jerks from rest).

# Sammy Line

Measured on 17 Icons8 Sammy Line animations with their source SVGs (Lottie).

**Sammy Line is the line version of Sammy** (`sammy.md`): the same animations with the same timing, drawn with thin dark outlines, white fills and two accent colours (dark teal and lilac) instead of the bright flat palette. The measurements match Sammy: 30 fps, loops 2–6 s, quick comic swings (~10 frames, ~10°), hops, pops from 0 (66 in 8), replacement drawings (15 of 17), lettering that writes itself.

Use `sammy.md` for all rules; this file only overrides the look.

## Differences from Sammy

- Thin outlines are everywhere (live strokes in 17 of 17): outline them before export, or the drawing loses its lines.

# Cyborg

**Preliminary**: measured on 2 Icons8 Cyborg animations with their SVGs.

Cyborg is bold graphic illustration in hot pink, mint and navy: a robot guard with a raygun and strobe lights, a cartoon brain climbing a growth chart.

## Format and motion

- Loops of **1.3 s** - short, punchy cycles (one file at 30 fps, one at 300 fps).
- Fast swings: ~10–20 frames, rotations ~16° (11–49°), moves ~34 px; things flash in and out with fades (68) and pops (14).
- Walking robot: legs on spring coils, strobe cones swing around; the brain hops up the chart, sweating, with a briefcase.

# Looney

Measured on 13 Icons8 Looney animations with their SVGs. Durations in frames at 30 fps, ranges p25–p75, motion measured per frame.

Looney is **1930s rubber-hose cartoon in black, white, grey and red**: a pie-eyed guy with a red quiff, gloved noodle arms, a mailbox that comes alive, a pyramid with an eye, a T-rex in a crown, an axe chopping spam. Fast, wacky slapstick.

## Format

- 30 fps. **Short loops: 2–3.3 s** (1.1–3.8). Mostly continuous; a gag can pause (17 % and 8 %).

## Motion

| What | Frames | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Rotation swing | 14 (8–20) | 20° (10–35°) |
| Move swing | 18 (9–25) | 30 px (15–60) |

- **Wild, elastic motion**: big swings of 20–35° in 8–20 frames; arms stretch like hoses, bodies bounce, eyes and mouths pop huge (morph in 13 of 13).
- Gags: the guy bites a cookie and chews with bulging eyes, a satellite dish head cracks and breaks, a courier runs with a burning parcel, a man and a mushroom pop out of a hole and wave then vanish, an axe chops spam letters dripping black goo, a mailbox opens and a letter waves goodbye.
- Replacement drawings in 8 of 13 (up to 266); lines draw on in 11 of 13.

## Easing

- Easy Ease 31 %, linear 18 % of authored keys.

## Engine limits and what to do

- Rubber-hose arms - bones with several joints and lag; bounce with squash - `bounce`/`spring`.
- Facial gags need poses; strokes in all 13 - outline before export.

# Slot kits

Start from the list of slots the deliverable needs, not from the first
illustration. Style choice depends on covering the whole list: a style that nails
the hero and has nothing for the empty state is the wrong style.

A slot is a place in the product, not a search word. The search word comes from
`VOCABULARY.md`, which maps the slot to the subject Icons8 actually drew.

**Every slot carries the product's own noun.** The kits below are skeletons: the
product fills them in. "Empty state" on a houseplant app is an empty pot or a
bare shelf, on an invoicing tool an empty tray, on a warehouse product an empty
rack: the noun sits in the missing container, not in a happy owner. Searching the state alone returns pictures about interfaces
in general, and in testing that produced a browser window on a plant app: right
category, wrong product, and nobody catches it in review because it is not
technically wrong.

## Why slots and not words

The most common searches inside icons8.com/illustrations are product states, in
order of volume over 12 months: login (770), empty (659), error (558), success
(550), 404 (524), check (510), welcome (401). People come looking for a place in
their product. The catalog, though, is organised by subject: business, objects,
decoration, people, artificial intelligence, web element. The skill exists to
translate between the two.

## Kits

### SaaS landing page (6-8 slots)

hero · three feature blocks · social proof or integrations · pricing accent ·
final call to action

Hero carries the aspect ratio for the page; feature blocks must share one crop.
Default styles: 3D Casual Life (broad), 3D Enterprise (B2B), Hand Drawn
Animation (human tone).

### Onboarding and first run (4-6 slots)

welcome · connect your data · invite the team · first result · done

Sequence matters more here than anywhere: the same character or object family
should carry through all steps. Reject a candidate that is right on its own but
introduces a new protagonist.

### Empty and error states (6 slots)

empty list · no search results · 404 · access denied · connection lost ·
something went wrong

These are the highest-demand slots in the whole catalog and the ones where
literal search fails hardest. They should read calm, not dramatic: the user is
already stuck. One object or one person, never a crowd.

### Product marketing site (8-10 slots)

hero · problem · solution · three capabilities · security · integrations ·
testimonial accent · footer call to action

The shield metaphor is flagged as a gap subject in the catalog's own list,
while the broad `security` query is well covered (2260). Check the specific
metaphor the slot needs before locking a style.

### Presentation deck (5-7 slots)

title · agenda · problem · approach · results · next steps

Readable at projector distance: large single objects, no fine linework.
Illustrations at 3D styles work; hand-drawn with thin lines does not.

### Blog and editorial (3-5 slots)

article header · two or three inline spots · newsletter block

Journal and Clip are built for this. Inline spots must be visually quieter than
the header or they compete with the text.

### Docs and help centre (4-6 slots)

getting started · configuration · troubleshooting · empty state · success ·
contact support

Monochrome or two-tone line art, small. Docs are dense, and a saturated flat
style reads as marketing next to a code block even when every subject is right:
that is exactly what a blind review called out in testing. Think of these as
punctuation for the text, not as pictures the reader stops at.

## Coverage check before you lock a style

Run one search per slot against the candidate style and count hits. Two or three
slots out of a dozen will be missing in any style, exactly as with icon packs.
If more than a quarter of the slots come back empty or wrong, the style is wrong
for this project, not the catalog.

Subjects the catalog covers well: business, people, ai, calendar, education,
laptop, phone, technology, earth, robot, healthcare, security, food, office,
work, box.

Subjects known to be thin (check before promising them): emotions of any kind
(sad, empathy, angry, fear, gratitude), health specifics (meditation, anxiety,
ultrasound, pediatrician), resume, review, feedback, checklist, onboarding,
survey, poverty, tiktok.
